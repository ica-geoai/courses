# Advanced Machine Learning for Geospatial Sciences

## Content

Building on the foundational concepts introduced in the Machine Learning for Geospatial Sciences course, this advanced module explores the cutting edge of machine learning and deep learning techniques tailored to Earth observation and geospatial applications. Students will engage with state-of-the-art methods for analyzing spatial and spatio-temporal data to address complex, real-world challenges with rigour and precision. 

The course begins with a concise review of neural networks and deep learning architectures, including training methodologies and optimization algorithms such as stochastic gradient descent. From there, students will delve into advanced deep learning frameworks—particularly convolutional neural networks (CNNs)—and their application to geospatial image analysis tasks, including classification, semantic segmentation, object detection, instance segmentation, and regression. 

The course then covers spatio-temporal modelling techniques, including recurrent neural networks (RNNs), transformers, and vision transformers, enabling students to handle not only time-series satellite imagery but also a wide array of dynamic and heterogeneous geospatial datasets, including sensor data, mobility traces, land cover transitions, climate projections, and crowdsourced or social media geodata. In addition, students will gain insight into more specialized and emerging topics, such as graph neural networks (GNNs), semi-supervised learning and pretraining strategies, foundation models for geospatial tasks, and generative AI. Recognizing the growing importance of model transparency and data quality, the course integrates key concepts from explainable AI, uncertainty quantification, and data-centric AI. These topics emphasize machine learning models’ interpretability, trustworthiness, robustness, and reliability in geospatial contexts. 

Beyond the technical content, the course includes showcases of current research and practical applications, with a particular focus on how advanced machine learning contributes to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals. Students will also critically examine ethical and responsible AI considerations, including fairness, bias mitigation, and the societal impact of GeoAI technologies.

## Course Objectives

Building expertise in advanced machine learning algorithms and their application to geospatial and Earth observation data.

## Participation Requirements

- Machine Learning for Geospatial Application (or equivalent)
- Python programming
