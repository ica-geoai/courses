## Course Overview

6-day course (4 hours/day) covering GeoAI using Python, open-source tools, and optional ArcGIS integration. Focus on data acquisition, visualization, preprocessing, and transfer learning workflows.

## Course Outline

* **Day 1:** Introduction to GeoAI & environment setup
* **Day 2:** Data acquisition (NAIP imagery, building footprints)
* **Day 3:** Visualization & exploration with Leafmap
* **Day 4:** Preparing data for AI models (preprocessing, formats)
* **Day 5:** Deep learning & transfer learning workflows
* **Day 6:** Applications, case studies, and capstone planning
