# Location and AI: The Basics

## Description

This course includes practical examples and provides a clear overview of how artificial intelligence technology combined with location data can support business planning, decision-making and innovation.

Location and AI: The Basics is aimed specifically at business leaders and decision-makers in the EU. No prior experience of artificial intelligence or location data is required to take the course. The online course can be completed at your own pace, anywhere and anytime.

## Target audience

This course is for you if you:
- are in charge of data-driven management, the data economy, digitalisation or business development
- want to find out what GeoAI is and how it can benefit your business
- want to learn how to benefit from data, especially the combination of geospatial data and artificial intelligence (AI).
- are business leader in a startup, SME or public organisations and want to explore new trends and opportunities in the geospatial field.

## Topics 

- The benefits of combining AI and geospatial data in business
- Core concepts of AI and Geospatial Data
- Practical Geospatial applications
- Risks, regulations and ethical considerations
- Key success factors for using location data & location Intelligence in business

# Location and AI: Deep Dive

## Description

This online course helps bridge the gap between AI and geospatial data offering a practical deep dive into some of the most common AI applications in the geospatial domain, from satellite imagery and spatial time series to point clouds. 

You'll explore open-source data and learn how models like SAM and GeoLLMs are transforming the way geospatial data is being analyzed. Based on real-world examples, the course demonstrates how organizations are using AI to tackle challenges in geospatial domain and concludes with emerging trends and future directions in Europe and globally. 

## Target audience

Location and AI: Deep Dive is aimed at professionals working with AI and/or location data in either the private or public sectors including: 
- AI/ML professionals expanding into the geospatial field 
- Data scientists 
- Software developers 
- Digital twin developers & city planners 
- Environmental analysts & researchers 
- Geospatial and GIS professionals 
- Other experts working with location-based data 

## Topics

- Different data types
- Common AI and Foundation models
- Practical GeoAI applications
- Assessing the trustworthiness of AI-generated geospatial data
- Future directions of GeoAI and how EU is supporting AI development and innovations
