# Advanced Geospatial Artificial Intelligence

## Course Description
This course delves into the deep learning aspect of geospatial artificial intelligence (GeoAI), with strong emphasis on geospatial computer vision. Students gain hands-on experience with PyTorch in order to apply GeoAI principles for extracting valuable information from geospatial data.


## Course Learning Outcomes
1. Introduction to Geospatial Deep Learning: Understand the principles and applications of deep learning in the context of geospatial data, including convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for image analysis.
2. Integration of Remote Sensing and GeoAI: Integrate remote sensing data with machine learning algorithms for geospatial analysis.
3. Develop models for land cover classification, change detection, and other applications.
4. Case Studies and Real-world Applications: Discuss real-world applications of GeoAI through case studies, exploring how these technologies are employed in fields such as environmental monitoring, urban planning, and disaster response.
5. Analyze and interpret real-world case studies applying GeoAI to address geospatial challenges.
6. Propose innovative solutions for new applications or problem domains.
7. Gain hands-on experience with industry-standard tools for remote sensing and GeoAI.
8. Develop practical skills relevant to employment in research or industry.
9. Emerging Trends in Remote Sensing and GeoAI: Stay informed about current and emerging trends in remote sensing and GeoAI.
10. Discuss potential future developments and their impact on research and industry.
