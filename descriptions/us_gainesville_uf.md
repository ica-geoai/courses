# GeoAI – Geographic Artificial Intelligence

## Course Description

Students will learn fundamental concepts and widely used methodologies for GeoAI. The integration of Geography and Artificial Intelligence (AI), referred to as GeoAI (a subfield of spatial data science), provides innovative approaches for addressing a wide range of geospatial problems in both natural environments and human society.

All laboratory sessions are hands-on and use real-world geospatial datasets to explore AI topics including:

- Linear models
- Tree-based methods
- Spatial cross-validation
- Super learning
- Deep learning
- Interpretable AI

Students will work with both physical and social science datasets and utilize the University of Florida’s supercomputer, HiPerGator, for computational analysis tasks.

The course integrates perspectives from the physical and social sciences to:

- Define and explain Geographic Artificial Intelligence
- Explore the types of questions that can be addressed using GeoAI
- Apply GeoAI methods to real-world geospatial data

## Student Learning Objectives

The primary objective of this course is to develop student knowledge and understanding of the principles, techniques, and applications of Geographic Artificial Intelligence.

Hands-on assignments encourage exploration, creativity, and practical understanding.

By the end of the course, students will be able to:

- Describe the historical development and foundations of GeoAI
- Explain the relationships between AI, machine learning, and deep learning
- Develop expertise in widely used GeoAI and machine learning tools and technologies
- Apply GeoAI techniques to different geographic questions and geospatial datasets
- Discuss why geography and GIS are essential for AI solutions addressing real-world problems
- Design and deliver presentations on state-of-the-art GeoAI methods and current limitations
- Demonstrate effective oral communication using real-world GeoAI application examples
