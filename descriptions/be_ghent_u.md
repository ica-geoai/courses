# Geospatial Artificial Intelligence

## Keywords

- Artificial Intelligence (AI)
- Geospatial Analysis
- Neural Networks (ANNs, CNNs, RNNs)
- Machine Learning (ML)
- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- Data Processing and Ethics

## Position of the Course

In this course, students will delve into the fascinating integration of artificial intelligence (AI) within the field of geography. The course not only provides a comprehensive overview of the principles and concepts behind AI, but also enables students to gain practical experience through interactive applications with various AI technologies and algorithms.

Moreover, students will learn how these advanced tools can be used to identify, analyze, and address complex geographical issues and challenges.

## Contents

### 1. Knowledge-Based AI: Qualitative Spatial Reasoning

- From quantitative to qualitative reasoning; time, space, and space-time concepts
- Fundamentals:
  - JEPD relations
  - Composition Tables
  - Conceptual Neighborhoods
- Major calculi:
  - Interval Algebra
  - 9-Intersection
  - RCC
  - QTC with variants

### 2. Knowledge-Based AI: Knowledge Graphs

- RDF, JSON-LD, Turtle
- Semantics via triples and SHACL validation
- OSLO and Linked Data Event Streams for semantic interoperability
- Applications in:
  - RAG
  - GraphRAG
  - Structured querying via SPARQL

### 3. Data-Driven AI: Neural Networks & Optimization

- Basics of neural networks, activation functions, and backpropagation
- Optimization:
  - Gradient descent
  - Regularization
  - Learning rate strategies
- Preventing overfitting:
  - Dropout
  - Weight initialization
  - Data augmentation

### 4. Data-Driven AI: Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs)

- RNN concepts and unrolling
- Vanishing/exploding gradient issues
- LSTM and GRU architectures for modeling sequential data
- Implementation and use in time series prediction

### 5. Data-Driven AI: Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)

- Vision theory and evolution toward deep learning for imagery
- Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and learned feature hierarchies
- Application to remote sensing:
  - Scene classification
  - Hands-on CNN exercise

### 6. Data-Driven AI: Transformers and LLMs

- Transformer architecture and attention mechanisms in GeoAI
- Applications of Large Language Models in spatial tasks
- Generative AI beyond text:
  - Multimodal examples
  - Practical use cases

## Initial Competences

Experience in programming (preferably Python) and in handling geospatial data.

## Final Competences

1. Understand the fundamentals of GeoAI and its main techniques and algorithms.
2. Gain hands-on experience in various AI techniques and algorithms relevant to geospatial analysis using the Python programming language.
3. Apply AI techniques to address or solve simple geographical problems and issues.

## Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI)

With the rise of advanced AI tools, their integration into GIS applications is becoming increasingly inevitable. This course acknowledges the potential of AI, including large language models (LLMs). Students are encouraged to use these AI tools if they wish.

Two simple forms of transparency are required:

- Indicate when and where you used an AI tool in your GIS assignment.
- Specify which tool or platform you used, and which specific model.

Think of this as giving credit, similar to citing a helpful post on Stack Overflow or GIS Stack Exchange. By being transparent, students uphold academic integrity and encourage deeper discussions about the convergence of GIS and AI.
